\textbf{AutoTex} | \textit{C++} \hfill Master Thesis

An \textit{LLVM}-based compiler framework for GPU memory-layout optimization.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, partopsep=0pt, parsep=0pt, leftmargin=*]
    \item Augmented the compilation pipeline to enable program analysis across the host-device boundary.
    \item Reduced fetched sectors by 38\% at L1, 15\% at L2, and 21\% at DRAM across 17 kernels, achieving \(2.4\times\) geomean speedup, by developing LLVM passes that detect access/layout mismatches and map data to \textit{texture memory}.
